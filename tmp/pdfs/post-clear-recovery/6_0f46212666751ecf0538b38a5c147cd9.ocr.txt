BANTILAN Absent: NONE Punong Barangay Kagawad Kagawad Kagawad Kagawad Kagawad Kagawad SK Chairman Barangay Treasurer Barangay Secretary Resolution No. - 2024 A RESOLUTION DECLARING PORTIONS OF BARANGAY SEVEN (7), THIS CITY, UNDER THE STATE OF CALAMITY DUE TO THE CONFLAGRATION THAT OCCURRED THEREAT LAST AUGUST 4, 2026. WHEREAS, on August 4, 2026, a devastating fire struck portions of Barangay 7, this City, causing extensive destruction to residential houses, business establishments, and public properties, leaving one hundred twenty-four (124) fire victims homeless aed displaced; WHEREAS, the fire also damaged essential barangay facilities and equipment, including the barangay fire truck and offce equipment, thereby affecting the barangays capability to deliver basic public services and emergency response; WHEREAS, the extent of the damage greatly affected the social and economic well-being Of the affected residents and required immediate intervention, rehabilitation, and recovery efforts; WHEREAS, pursuant to Republic Act No. 10121 (Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act 2010) and other applicable laws and regulations, the Sangguniang Barangay may recommend the declaration of a State of Calamity to facilitate the immediate provision of emergency assistance, rehabilitation, and utilization of available calamity funds, subject to existing rules and regulations; WHEREFORE, on motion jointly moved and seconded by members present, on their session assembled, it was; RESOLVED, as it is hereby RESOLVED, to declare Barangay 7 under a State of Calamity due to the devastating fire that occurred on August 4, 2026, subject to the recommendation of the Barangay Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Committee (BDRRMC) and the confirmation/approval of the appropriate authorities as required by law. RESOLVED finally, that a copy of this Resolution be furnished to the City Social Welfare and Development Office and the City Disaster Risk Reduction Management Office for their information and appropriate action. Done this 5th day of August, 2026 @ Barangay 7, Cagayan de Oro City. I/WE HEREBY CERTIFY TO THE CORRECTNESS OF THE ABOVE RESOLUTION. Attested b ALD V. SERINA nong Barangay ELDAYW.
