WHEREAS, the Philippine Constitution recognizes the vital role of the youth in nation-building and mandates the promotion and protection of their physical, moral, spiritual, intllectual, and social well-being; WHEREAS, pursuant to Presidential Decree No. 603 (The Child and YOUth Welfare Code), parents have the natural right and primary duty to rear, care for, and discipline their children to ensure they become responsible and productive members of society; WHEREAS, under Republic Act No. 9344 (Juvenile Justice and Welfare Act of 2006), local govemment units are mandated to implement local juvenile intervention programs and protection measures for "Children at Risk," including curfew hours, to protect them from neglect, exploitation, and criminal vulnerabilities; WHEREAS, the inhalation of rugby and other volatile substances under Republic Act No. 9165 (Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002) and Presidential Decree No. 1619 inflicts severe damage on the mental and physical health of minors and serves as a major catalyst for petty crimes and public disturbances in the community; WHEREAS, it has been observed that the primary cause of minors wandering the streets at late hours and engaging in substance abuse is the lack of proper parental guidance, supervision, and strict discipline from their parents or guardians; WHEREAS, under Section 391 of Republic Act No. 7160 (Local Government Code of 1991), the Sangguniang Barangay has the power to enact ordinances intended to maintain public order, safety, and the general welfare of its constituents; NOW, THEREFORE, the Sangguniang Barangay of Macabalan, in a formal session assembled, hereby ordains: SECTION 1. TITLE. This ordinance shall be known as the "Parental Accountability and Youth Protection Ordinance of Barangay Macabalan". SECTION 2. DEFINITION OF TERMS. Minor: Any person below eighteen (18) years of age. • Parent/Guardian: The biological father or mother, foster parents, or any adult person who has actual custody, care, and temporary or permanent charge over the minor.
