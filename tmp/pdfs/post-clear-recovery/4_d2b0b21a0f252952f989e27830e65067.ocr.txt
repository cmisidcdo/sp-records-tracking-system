To Subject Date REPUBLIC OF THE PHILIPPINES City of Cagayan de Oro OFFICE OF THE CITY MAYOR ALL DEPARTMENT MANAGERS/CHIEFS OF OFFICE AUTHORITY TO ALLOW ALL CASUAL OR JOB ORDER PERSONNEL TO RENDER EXTRA SERVICES August 3, 2026 Due to exigency in the service, you are hereby directed to allow all casual or job order personnel under your respective offices to render extra services onsite or under work-from-home arrangement, equivalent to four (4) working days within the month of August 2026. This is also to allow these personnel to complete the twenty-two (22) working days of public service within the month. In observance of the Energy Conservation Protocols, extra services onsite shall be conducted for a maximum of four (4) days only for each office. You are directed to assign official dates for this. In addition, you are required to issue an Office Order listing the names Of the casual or job order personnel authorized to render extra services. Those assigned to work-from home shall be required to use the work-from-home biometrics system as proof of their attendance, and they shall remain reachable during office hours through official communication channels, including attendance to meetings whenever necessary. This Office Order shall form part of the documentation for payroll purposes. As such, the authorized personnel may claim their respective salaries which shall be charged against the respective office's appropriations, subject, however, to the accounting and auditing laws, rules, and regulations.
